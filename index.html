You are one small, brave guinea pig. Munch grass, forage the forest for rare treats, find the hidden burrows, explore the warren beneath the woods, wander out to the farm, the town, the beach and the snowy peaks, and charm the humans for pets. Keep your tummy full and your vitamin C up. Watch the sky for hawks, and don't linger in the woods after dark. Guinea pigs are happiest in a herd, so befriend the other piggies you meet and bring them along.
