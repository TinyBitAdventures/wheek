# Wheek!

A guinea pig survival forage in the big green woods. Pick your guinea pig, munch grass, forage the forest for rare treats, find the hidden burrows, explore the warren of tunnels beneath the woods and charm the humans for pets. Keep your tummy full and your vitamin C up, watch the sky for hawks and don't linger in the woods after dark. Guinea pigs are happiest in a herd, so befriend the other piggies you meet and bring them along.

Built with Three.js. Every model is generated by a Blender script. No build step.

**Play it:** [tinybitadventures.com](https://tinybitadventures.com/games/wheek/) · What's new: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|--------|------|
| Scurry around | WASD |
| Sprint (costs energy) | Shift |
| Eat, enter a tunnel, gnaw a twig | E (hold to nibble grass; while gnawing, press E when the marker is in the green) |
| Forage | Hold F |
| Sniff out treats and tunnels | R |
| Wheek at a human for a veggie | Q |
| Popcorn (when happy) | Space |
| Chat and befriend a piggy | Hold C |
| Big map | M |
| Forager's journal | J |
| Pause, save, settings, quit to the title | P or Esc |
| Look around, zoom | Drag the mouse, mouse wheel |

**Touch screens:** put a thumb down on the left of the screen and slide to scurry; drag on the right to look around and pinch to zoom. Round buttons wheek, sniff, hop (popcorn) and scurry, and the map, journal and pause buttons sit at the top. Whenever there's something to do (eat, forage, chat, enter a tunnel, wheek at a human) a button for it pops up on the right: tap it, or hold it to munch, forage and chat.

**Gamepads:** left stick to scurry, right stick to look, Ⓐ eat and enter, Ⓧ forage, Ⓨ chat, Ⓑ popcorn, RB sniff, LB wheek, RT scurry, Back for the map, d-pad up for the journal, Start to pause. In menus the d-pad (or the stick) moves between buttons, Ⓐ picks and Ⓑ goes back. The prompts show the gamepad's buttons while you're using one.

The park sits in the middle of a 3×3 world of zones (a snowy mountain, a deep wood, a town, a beach, a creek, a petting zoo, a farm and a sunflower maze): walk off any edge, sides or corners, to go to the next one. Stand still next to a kneeling human for pets. At a burrow, press E to nap, crawl down into the warren or scurry along a route you have already explored. The barns at the farm and the petting zoo have little holes at the foot of their walls: squeeze in to munch the hay pile or burrow into it (F) for the treats hidden inside. After 6 PM, the pet shop downtown has a cat flap round the back: tiptoe past Duchess the cat, crawl through the tubes (E at an open end) and make friends with Butterscotch. At the top of Willow Creek a big leaf waits on the bank: ride it down the creek (steer with A/D, paddle with W, slow with S) to Willow Island. In Sunflower Fields, slip into the old windmill, dodge the sweep arm, climb to the loft and catch the seeds spilling from the hopper (A/D). In the Deep Wood, squeeze into the giant hollow oak and hop up the bracket fungi inside to the lookout at the top, without waking the owl. In Critter Corner, duck under the flap of the striped agility tent and run the course against the clock for a rosette (E on the green mat to start, Space to hop the bar). The title screen has three save slots. Save from the pause menu (P), or "Save & quit" back to the title; the game also autosaves at sunrise and when you close the tab. After a game over you can go back to your last save. Saves and your best score live in the browser's local storage. **Settings** (⚙️ on the title screen or in the pause menu) set the volume, music and sound effects, the graphics (Low turns off shadows, thins the fur and draws less far away, for slower computers and phones), screen flashes and whether to show the touch controls.

## Run it locally

Serve the folder with any static web server and open it:

    python3 -m http.server 8000     # then open http://localhost:8000

The models load with `fetch`, so opening `index.html` straight from disk won't work. Three.js and the Fredoka font load from CDNs.

## How it's put together

| Path | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| `index.html` | Title screen, breed picker, HUD, journal and menus (markup and CSS). The version lives in its script tag, `js/game.js?v=X.Y.Z`, which also busts caches |
| `js/game.js` | The game: the seeded forest (the same every time), the guinea pig and herd, humans, hawk and foxes, foraging, the eight other zones (recipes for one shared builder, each built from its own seed the first time you visit), the burrows and the warren underneath (a cave mesh built at load from a distance field), day and night, sound effects and the HUD |
| `assets/*.glb` | The models, generated by `tools/build_assets.py` |
| `assets/music/*.mp3` | The music: three seamless loops (the meadow by day, moonlight at night, the warren underground) composed for the game with [Wavelength](https://github.com/austinginder/wavelength). The game loops each one sample-exactly and cross-fades between them |
| `tools/build_assets.py` | The Blender script that builds every model |
| `tools/preview.py` | Renders one model to a PNG |
| `tools/shots.cjs` | A screenshot tour of the game with Playwright |
| `tools/check.cjs` | World checks with Playwright: everything reachable in every zone and the warren, night, random input, eating, saves, memory |

`window.__game` is a debug handle (state, pig, herd, humans, tunnels, camera) that the screenshot tour uses to pose scenes.

## Models

    blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/build_assets.py -- assets             # every model
    blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/build_assets.py -- assets Fox Oak     # just these
    blender -b --factory-startup --python tools/preview.py -- Fox 1.4 0.35 prev.png  # a quick look at one

A new model also needs its name in `MODELS` in `js/game.js`. Each model has its own random seed, so rebuilding one gives the same geometry as a full build (the bytes of the `.glb` can still shift). 14 of the shipped models were exported before that seed and the script's last tweaks, so rebuilding one of them gives a slightly different variation: check it in the game before committing.

## Checks

    PW=/path/to/node_modules/playwright node tools/check.cjs              # every check
    PW=/path/to/node_modules/playwright node tools/check.cjs reach saves  # just these

Runs in a tiny headless window and tests the world through `window.__game`: from every way into each zone, a walkability grid (the game's own collision, deep water and the rim) must reach every exit, burrow, forage spot, plant, human and animal; the same underground for every chamber, curio and treat. Then night in every zone, random key mashing, eating and foraging, every zone's signature treat (and wading in to forage watercress), goals and a human's request, the music (the loops decode and follow day, night and the warren) and the settings, continuing old and zoned saves (and a save round trip), touch controls on a phone-sized screen, a stand-in gamepad from the menus into the game, and memory after visiting everything. Exits 1 on any failure or console error.

## Screenshots

    PW=/path/to/node_modules/playwright node tools/shots.cjs shots              # every scene
    PW=/path/to/node_modules/playwright node tools/shots.cjs shots herd forage  # just these

Poses the title, breed picker, herd, pets, forage, house, tunnel and journal (and the warren, zones, each zone's signature forage spot, a zone guinea pig, the places checklist), then prints draw calls, triangles and any console errors. `URL` points it at another copy (it defaults to the local dev site, https://wheek.localhost/), `W`/`H` set the size, and `TOUCH=1` emulates a touch screen (the `touchui` scene shows the touch controls).
