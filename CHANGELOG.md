# Changelog

## Unreleased

### The Warren
- The burrows now open into a warren you can explore: winding tunnels under the whole wood, laid out to match the map above, so every burrow's den sits right under its entrance. Crawl in from any burrow you've found (E, then "Crawl down into the warren").
- Five hidden chambers to discover: the Glowworm Grotto, Root Cellar, Mushroom Hall, Old Seed Store and Crystal Hollow, each with its own look and treats to eat.
- A curio stash: nine lost trinkets tucked into the chambers and dead-end nooks, with the Golden Acorn waiting in the deepest one. They get their own page in the journal.
- Your map fills in as you explore. Press M for a big map; sniff (R) underground to find curios and mark unexplored chambers.
- Walk to a burrow's den from below to discover it, and climb out there. Passages you've walked end to end become quick routes: from a burrow, you can scurry straight to any burrow you've linked up.
- Your herd comes underground with you. Daylight (or moonlight) falls down each burrow's shaft, and the hawk and foxes can't reach you down there.

## v0.1.0 (2026-09-28)

The first release. Wheek! moves out of the playground into its own repo.

### The game
- You are one small guinea pig in a big green wood. Munch grass, forage 15 treats from common to legendary (and one that's toxic), find 10 hidden tunnels and travel between them, and charm the humans for pets and veggies.
- Survival: health, fullness, vitamin C, energy and happiness. A hawk circles overhead, so dive for cover, and foxes prowl the woods at night.
- Pick your guinea pig: American, Abyssinian, Peruvian or Skinny Pig, each with its own coats and a perk, and give it a name.
- Nine wild guinea pigs live in the meadow and the woods. Chat with them to bring up to six into your herd. They follow you, huddle when you stand still and raise your score.
- A forager's journal of the treats you've found and your herd. Your guinea pig and best score are saved.

### Models
- Every model is generated by a Blender script: guinea pigs, humans, the hawk and fox, trees, plants, treats, the house and garden.
- Layered fur on every guinea pig.

### Moving in
- The single-file playground build is now `index.html`, `js/game.js` and `assets/`, with the models loaded as separate files.
- The version shows on the title screen.
- `tools/`: the Blender model builder (each model now has its own random seed), a model preview and a screenshot tour.
